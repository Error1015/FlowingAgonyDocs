# About

**MarbleGate's Exotic Enchantment: Flowing Agony: Reborn** is a remake and port of [MarbleGate's Exotic Enchantment: Flowing Agony](https://www.mcmod.cn/class/3872.html), ported and maintained by [Error1015](https://www.mcmod.cn/author/34326.html). The original author is [白门 (MarbleGate)](https://www.mcmod.cn/author/24436.html).

The mod is built around the enchantment system and aims to extend vanilla enchanting: by adding new enchantment content it opens up more possibilities for gear progression, combat and adventuring. Players can explore and combine different enchantment effects to build weapons and armour that match their own playstyle.
